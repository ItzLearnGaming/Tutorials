Throttle Stop Tutorial


This is a tutorial on how to use Throttle Stop to optimize your CPU performance. This application allow you to undervolt your CPU to get better FPS in Gaming by reducing thermal throttling.

  1. Download and install Throttle Stop from the official website down below.
  2. Open Throttle stop as administrator.
  3. Click Turn on button in the bottom of the window.
  4. Click on the FIVR button.
  5. Click on CPU core in the FIVR Control Section
  6. Tick the box for Unlock Adjustable Voltage.
  7. Now Change the offset Voltage to -50 or lower.
  8. Click on CPU cache in the FIVR Control Section
  9. Tick the box for Unlock Adjustable Voltage.
  10. Now Change the offset Voltage to -50 or lower.
  11. Click OK-Save Voltages immediately in Save Voltage Section.
  12. Click Apply and Ok.
  13. Then Click Save
  • Make sure to Run Throttle Stop Manually
